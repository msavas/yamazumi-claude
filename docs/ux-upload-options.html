Considerations:

  • Extra click to start (button → modal)
  • Modal overlay might feel like extra step

Pros:

  • One less click (direct drag-and-drop)
  • Visual transformation is satisfying
  • Upload area shows exactly where video will appear
  • More immediate, less friction

Considerations:

  • Takes up screen space when empty
  • Might feel less "polished" than modal

💡 Recommendation

Option B (Upload Area on Screen) aligns better with Direction 2 (Dense Dashboard) and provides a more direct, friction-free experience. The visual transformation from upload area to video player is satisfying and shows users exactly where their video will appear. For a tool focused on speed and efficiency (15-minute goal), reducing clicks and friction is valuable.
